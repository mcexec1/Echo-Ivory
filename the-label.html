The Label

A label built around a simple idea.

A great record and a great rollout are two different disciplines. Echo & Ivory exists to take both seriously, at the same time, for every artist on the roster.

Where the Name Comes From

Echo & Ivory isn't a genre. It's a working relationship between two instincts.

01

Echo

The part of a record that can't be manufactured — a voice, a lyric, a feel that stays with a listener after the song ends. This is protected first, above every other decision.

02

Ivory

The craft and the framework around it — production, positioning, release strategy, and the infrastructure that gets a record in front of the right ears. Precision built around the instinct, never in place of it.

How We Operate

What that looks like day to day.

A small set of principles, applied to every artist and every release.

01

Song First, Always

No release moves forward until the song itself earns it. Strategy supports the music — it never gets ahead of it.

02

Built for Today's Ears

Release plans are built around how music is actually discovered and consumed now, not how the industry used to work.

03

Artist Ownership

Creative control, transparency, and ownership stay with the artist. The label builds the machine around the art.

04

Catalog Over Moments

Every release is treated as part of a longer arc — building a catalog and a career meant to hold up over time.

The Structure

Part of a larger portfolio.

Echo & Ivory operates under Hidden Grove Music LLC and sits inside the wider Common Thread Holdings Co. portfolio — independent in its A&R and creative decisions, backed by the operational stability of an established multi-industry holding company.
