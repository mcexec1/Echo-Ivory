The Founder

Founder, Echo & Ivory

Echo & Ivory was founded by Michael "Wayne" Conner, whose relationship with the music industry started long before the label did. Close to twenty years spent inside A&R rooms and marketing departments taught him what most catalogs never learn firsthand: that a great record and a great rollout are two different disciplines, and a label only works when it takes both seriously.

That experience runs from artist and repertoire work at Avatar Records, to VP-level A&R and marketing leadership at MIH Entertainment Group, to hands-on operations and artist management at Ashtray Management. Across all three, the same lesson kept repeating — the songs have to be real, and the strategy around them has to be built for how people actually discover music today, not how they used to.

Echo & Ivory, operating under Hidden Grove Music LLC, is where those two decades come together as their own label: a home for artists who want a real creative partner on the analog side and a real strategist on the digital side, in the same room.

The Philosophy

Where the label draws its name.

Echo & Ivory isn't a genre — it's a working relationship between two instincts.

01

Echo

The part of the process that can't be manufactured — a voice, a lyric, a feel that sticks with a listener long after the song ends. This is the analog heart of the label: real songwriting and real performance, protected first.

02

Ivory

The craft and the framework — production, positioning, release strategy, and the digital infrastructure that gets a record in front of the right ears. This is the digital soul: precision built around the instinct, never in place of it.

03

Under Hidden Grove Music

Echo & Ivory operates under Hidden Grove Music LLC and sits inside the wider Common Thread Holdings Co. portfolio — independent in its A&R decisions, backed by an operator who has built and run businesses across media, retail, and finance.
